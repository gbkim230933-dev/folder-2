About Son Heungmin

Son Heungmin is a very famous Asian soccer player. He is famous for being one of the best Asian soccer players. He was sent to Germany when he was only 16. His father raised him with a Spartan-style upbringing. We remember Son Heungmin as a trailblazing global football icon who redefined Asian representation in elite sports through historic achievements and an exemplary character.

More about Son Heungmin...
